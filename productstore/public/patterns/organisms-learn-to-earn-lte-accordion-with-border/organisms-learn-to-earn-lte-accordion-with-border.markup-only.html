Every month, you can earn up to 15% of your product points in Loyalty Shopping Dollars. These rewards will be automatically deposited into your account after checkout. Loyalty Shopping Dollars can be redeemed as they are activated.

Earn a Higher Percent when You Shop Early in the Month.

Earn 15% when you shop before the 20th of the month and 10% after.

Once you have earned dollars, you can activate them by watching qualifying videos anywhere on the website. Simply look for the dollar amount in the upper left-hand corner to know how much you can activate. When you watch the whole video and answer a few questions, you’ll activate those dollars you have earned.

Loyalty Shopping Dollars cannot be used to purchase specials, packs, products without points, discounted items, and sales aids; or to pay for shipping, handling or taxes.
Details about the dollars you earned can be found on your order receipt or within My Account.
Loyalty Shopping Dollar redemptions can only occur with a minimum Product Point order and a $1 handling fee, applicable shipping, and taxes will apply. No Product Points will be given for products purchased in whole or in part with Loyalty Shopping Dollars. Loyalty Shopping Dollars cannot be used to pay for shipping, handling or tax on products.
