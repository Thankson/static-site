What we use, and why, in products that deliver amazing results.

  • Citric Acid Naturally found in citrus fruits. Effective alternative to harsh chemicals that dissolves hard water, soap scum, and mineral deposits. Included in Tub & Tile™ Bathroom Cleaner and Safe & Mighty™ Toilet Bowl Cleaner.

  • Thyme Oil Natural oil from the herb thyme. Proven to kill viruses and bacteria by making them spill their guts. Included in Sol-U-Guard® Botanical Disinfectant and Safe & Mighty™ Toilet Bowl Cleaner.

  • Melaleuca Oil Natural oil from the melaleuca alternafolia plant in Australia. Boosts cleaning performance and penetrates tough stains. Included in Sol-U-Mel® Stain Remover and Tough & Tender® All-Purpose Cleaner.

  • Glycolic Acid Naturally found in sugar beets. A safer alternative to use on common fixtures that dissolves hard water, soap scum, and mineral deposits. Included in Tub & Tile™ Bathroom Cleaner and No Work® Daily Shower Cleaner.

  • Sodium Citrate Mineral salt is derived from citric acid and occurs naturally in plants and animals. Natural, effective alternative to synthetic stabilizers. Included in MelaPower® and Lemon Brite®.

  • Essential Oils Variety of naturally sourced oils. Used in our blend of fragrance oils to provide an enjoyable scent experience. Included in all cleaners.
